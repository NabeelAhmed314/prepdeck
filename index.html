Privacy Policy — PrepDeck

Last updated: 10 August 2026

PrepDeck ("we", "us", "the app") respects your privacy. This document explains what data the app collects, why, and your rights regarding it.

PrepDeck has no user accounts. You never create a login, and your interview preps — the questions, the model answers and your study progress — are stored on your device and are never uploaded to us.

We do run one server. When you ask PrepDeck to generate questions, the text you submit passes through a service we operate before reaching the AI provider. That server does not keep the text; what it does keep is a small counter used to enforce the free-tier limit. Both are described in section 1.

A note about free-text fields

The job description and background fields are free text, and whatever you type into them is sent to our server and on to our AI provider (section 3) to generate your questions. Please do not paste information you would not want processed by a third-party AI service — for example another person's contact details, confidential material from your employer, or government identifiers.

2. How we use your data

  • To generate interview questions and model answers from the job description and background you provide.
  • To store your preps, questions and study progress locally on your device so they are there when you reopen the app.
  • To count how many question sets you have generated, so the free tier can be enforced, and to limit how many can be requested from one network per day so the service is not abused.
  • To determine whether you have an active subscription and to restore purchases you have already made.
  • To measure which features are used, so the app can be improved. This is optional and can be switched off.
  • To detect, diagnose and fix crashes and errors.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not build advertising profiles.

3. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. The following third-party services process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase (Supabase, Inc. — https://supabase.com/privacy) — hosts our generation server and its database, in the Tokyo, Japan region. It handles the job description and background text in transit, and stores the device identifier, generation counts and short-lived IP records described in section 1.
  • OpenRouter (OpenRouter, Inc. — https://openrouter.ai/privacy) — receives the job description, your background text, and the company / role / round you entered, forwarded from our server, in order to generate questions. OpenRouter routes that request to an underlying model provider, so the text is also processed by whichever model provider serves the request.
  • RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc. — https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy) — receives purchase and subscription events and an anonymous app user ID.
  • PostHog (PostHog, Inc. — https://posthog.com/privacy) — receives app usage events, a device identifier and your IP address. Only while analytics are enabled.
  • Sentry (Functional Software, Inc. — https://sentry.io/privacy/) — receives crash and error reports with device and app version information.
  • Google Play (Google LLC — https://policies.google.com/privacy) — processes the subscription payment itself. We never see or receive your card or payment details.

4. Your rights

You can:

  • Turn off analytics. Settings → Privacy → "Share usage analytics". When this is off, no usage events are sent to PostHog. Note that crash reports are still sent, because they are what makes a crash fixable.
  • Delete your preps. Swipe a prep on "My preps" and delete it. The prep and all of its questions are removed from your device immediately.
  • Delete everything on your device. Uninstalling the app — or using Android's Settings → Apps → PrepDeck → Clear data — permanently erases every prep, question, answer and the background text you wrote, along with the device identifier the app generated. There is no account for us to delete, because there is no account.
  • Request access or erasure of the data held about you. Email nabeelahmed.support@gmail.com and we will act on it. Please note the limit described below.

A limit you should know about. Because PrepDeck has no accounts, we have no way to connect a request to a particular record on our server. The device identifier lives only on your device; once you clear the app's data it is gone from your side, and the counter left on ours can no longer be matched to you or to anyone else. If you want us to erase the record and can still open the app, contact us and we will explain how to supply the identifier.

Records held by our analytics and crash-reporting processors are deleted within 30 days of such a request. IP records on our server are deleted automatically within a few days. Generation counters are kept for as long as the free-tier limit needs to be enforced, because a counter that expired would reset the free allowance; they contain no information about you beyond a random value and a number. Data stored on your device is under your control and is removed the moment you delete the app or clear its data.

5. Security

  • All network requests use HTTPS, so data is encrypted in transit.
  • Your preps and background text are stored in the app's private storage, which Android isolates from other apps on the device.
  • Any credentials the app needs to store locally are encrypted at rest with AES-GCM using a key held in the Android Keystore.
  • Our server does not store the job descriptions or background text it processes, so there is no archive of that content to breach. The database it does keep holds only random identifiers, counters and short-lived IP records, and is not readable by the app or by anyone using the app's public key.
  • Because there is no account and no server holding your preps, there is no central database of user prep content.

6. Children

PrepDeck is intended for adults preparing for professional technical interviews. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us at nabeelahmed.support@gmail.com and we will delete it.

7. International transfers / GDPR

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the following applies.

Legal basis. We process the job description and background text you submit on the basis of performance of a contract — you asked the app to generate questions, and it cannot do that without sending them. We process purchase data on the same basis. We process the device identifier, generation counters and IP records on the basis of our legitimate interest in enforcing the free tier and preventing abuse of a paid AI service. We process analytics on the basis of your consent, which you may withdraw at any time via Settings → Privacy. We process crash reports on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping the app working.

Transfers. Our generation server and its database are hosted in Japan (Tokyo). Transfers of personal data from the EEA to Japan are covered by the European Commission's adequacy decision for Japan, which recognises Japan as providing an equivalent level of data protection. Our other processors (OpenRouter, RevenueCat, PostHog, Sentry) are established in the United States and process data there; where required, those transfers rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or an equivalent approved mechanism operated by the provider. The model provider that ultimately serves a generation request may be located outside the EEA, and is engaged through OpenRouter on the same basis.

Your rights. You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and port your personal data, and to object to processing. Most of this you can exercise directly: your preps live on your device, so you can read, change and erase them yourself at any time. For anything held by us or by a processor, email nabeelahmed.support@gmail.com, subject to the identification limit in section 4. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Retention. Data on your device is kept until you delete it. IP records are deleted automatically within a few days. Generation counters persist as described in section 4. Analytics and crash records are retained by our processors for no more than 30 days after a deletion request, and are subject to those providers' own retention limits.
