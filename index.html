What it does

  • Questions drawn from the actual role

    Not a generic bank. The deck follows the stack, the scale and the responsibilities named in the posting in front of you.

  • A model answer on every card

    Each question also tells you why this role makes it likely, and the follow-up an interviewer would ask next.

  • Study as flashcards

    Tap a card to see the answer, swipe right when you are confident, left to flag it. Your second pass is only what you got wrong.

  • The whole loop, not just coding

    Technical depth, ML and data system design, behavioural, and role-specific questions, with a realistic difficulty spread.

  • No account, no sign-up

    There is no login. Your preps, questions and progress are stored on your device.

Free tier and subscription

Free gives you three decks and the first five questions in each. A subscription opens the rest of every deck and removes the limit on decks. Weekly or annual, billed through Google Play, cancel any time.
